# Lab 4 — Certification Campaigns

## Objective

Run three different access certification campaigns and produce the audit evidence that proves they happened.

## What I did

Configured and ran three campaign types, each with a different reviewer lens:

1. **Manager campaigns** — managers review their direct reports: should your people still have what they have?
2. **Entitlement-owner campaigns** — the owner of a specific entitlement reviews everyone holding it: should anyone have this access at all?
3. **Application-owner campaigns** — the person who owns the application is on the hook for all access into it.

Then generated the **certification reports** — because the deliverable is the evidence, not the campaign. Auditors want the export.

## Key concepts

- **Who reviews matters as much as what gets reviewed.** Each campaign type catches a different class of risk: managers catch stale per-person access, entitlement owners catch over-provisioned entitlements, app owners catch access that slipped past both.
- **A certification nobody can prove happened did not happen.** Reporting and evidence export are part of the control, not an afterthought.

## Skills demonstrated

Certification campaign configuration, reviewer assignment models, access review execution, compliance reporting and audit evidence generation.
